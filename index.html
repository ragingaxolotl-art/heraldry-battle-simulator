The five numbered shields match your country cards. On an uploaded map, type the country names and place the shields yourself. Names and terrain are not automatically read from a PNG. Add your own shield below, then drag it on the map—or focus it and use arrow keys. Moving a shield does not change borders. Clean PNG downloads exclude shields and arrows.     2 · Choose your countries

    Your Five Countries

    Use the land-location dropdown at the top of each card to choose five different countries on a generated map. Each marker moves to its chosen land, and troops and gold load for you. Then rename your countries, add shields, and use the map markers to adjust their positions. For an uploaded PNG, place the markers yourself because the picture does not contain a list of locations. Relationships are mutual.
